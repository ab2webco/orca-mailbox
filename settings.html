    Add an IMAP account

    Works with any mailbox that offers IMAP over TLS: cPanel hosting, Zoho, iCloud, Yahoo, Gmail with an app password. The password is typed in your browser on a page served by this computer, never in this panel.

    Only needed if your server does not mark its Trash folder. Used by a later version for safe deletes.

    Gmail and Outlook

    Sign-in with Google and Microsoft comes in the next version. Until then, Gmail can be added above as IMAP (imap.gmail.com, port 993) with an app password. Outlook and Microsoft 365 only accept Microsoft sign-in for IMAP, so they wait for that version.
